01 · The core loop

Five steps between an idea and a payment.

A proposal is not a payment. Each step stores its evidence, so the same inputs always produce the same outcome.

01

Constrain

Set allowed time windows, a timezone and daily or monthly caps. No constraints means no automatic approval.

02

Propose

Name a product, price and currency, by hand or prefilled from a calendar event.

03

Evaluate

Channel3 offers are cleaned and compared to the median. KERNEL adds a merchant trust signal.

04

Decide

Explicit rules return AUTO_APPROVE, MANUAL_REVIEW or BLOCK, with reason codes. No generative model.

05

Pay and audit

PayPal Sandbox order, authorize, then capture or void. Every step lands in an append-only log.

02 · Decision policy

Rules you can read, outcomes you can predict.

The proposed price is compared with the median of at least three usable Channel3 offers. Money is handled in integer cents.

03 · Payment state model

Reserve the money. Then decide to take it.

An authorization can be voided until it is captured. Capture and void compete through guarded transitions, so only one can ever succeed.
